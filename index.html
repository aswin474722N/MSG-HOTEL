About

The Story of MSG NIGHT KADAI

Sizzling tawas, aromatic spices, and late-night cravings.

MSG NIGHT KADAI brings alive the rich, bustling flavor of authentic night kadai cooking with a gourmet touch. Every meal is cooked fresh to order over open flames with freshly ground masalas.

  • Fresh cuts, hand-ground spices, and hot-off-the-tawa breads.
  • Late night service till 11:00 PM for all your midnight cravings.
  • Open outdoor ambience with fast, hygienic, and warm service.

Experience why hundreds of foodies visit MSG NIGHT KADAI every night for our signature gravies, crispy roasts, and hot special beverages.
